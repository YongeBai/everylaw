# Feature ideas — viral mechanics batch (Aug 25, 2026)

Extracted principles: one shared daily object (Wordle), rarity scoring that rewards
deep cuts (HoopGrids/Immaculate Grid), construction-under-constraints + a
perfect-run gauntlet (82-0), spoiler-free emoji share artifacts, streaks, and the
post-vote comparison moment as the bridge into discourse. Ten buildable features:

1. **Daily Trial share-card + streak.** Wordle-ify /docket: a spoiler-free emoji
   strip of your last 7 trial verdicts (⚖🟩🟥…) plus streak count, copyable in one
   tap. Polls close at midnight; the verdict publishes next morning — scarcity and
   a reason to come back.

2. **Rarity scoring.** Whenever a player names or picks a law (grids, dockets,
   arguments citing sections), show what % of everyone else made the same choice.
   Low % + valid = "deep cut" badge. A weekly "rarest finds" board rewards people
   for reading beyond the famous laws.

3. **The Perfect Term pick'em.** Before you vote on a law, predict how America
   ruled. Running record; 30 straight perfect days = a Perfect Term and a hall of
   fame entry. Every reveal shows the real split plus the best case from each side
   — prediction becomes a discourse funnel.

4. **LawGrids daily.** 3×3 criteria intersections (title × trait: "enacted before
   1960", "public leans dissolve", "under 700 words") generated from corpus
   metadata and validated server-side against the real 60k sections. Nine guesses,
   rarity-scored, emoji-grid share.

5. **Lawdle daily.** Guess the mystery statute in 6 from attribute feedback
   (title ↑↓, decade, word count, status). The solved law becomes that day's
   high-traffic vote page — the game IS the distribution channel for one law/day.

6. **Draft Your Code gauntlet.** Build a pocket constitution — N laws under a word
   cap — then your draft faces a week of public matchup votes. Undefeated codes
   are enshrined; a season leaderboard ranks drafters. The 82-0 heir: construction
   under constraints, then the gauntlet.

7. **The Reveal as default post-vote UX.** Everywhere a keep/dissolve exists:
   vote → "You + N% of America" → the other side's best arguments while the
   comparison is fresh → "fair point" / rebut (280 chars). Turns every vote into a
   discourse entry, sitewide. (From the Tinder-concept feedback.)

8. **The three tables.** Most Defended / Most Condemned / Most Contested (closest
   to 50/50 above a volume floor), with movement — plus a public JSON API and
   embeddable widgets so journalists and posters can cite "the margarine law is
   America's 5th most condemned" with a live source.

9. **Regulation Madness (annual event).** The 64 most-condemned laws, seeded from
   the leaderboard, one elimination round per week, public voting per matchup.
   Bracket picks are shareable and scored; the champion triggers a pre-written
   "petition your representative" template. Appointment-viewing for repeal.

10. **The Receipt generator.** Personalized share artifact: pick a routine (or
    your birthday) and get an itemized CVS-length receipt of the laws that
    silently applied — rendered as an image for posting, every line linking to
    that law's vote page. The absurd length is the message.

Interactive design studies for all ten mechanics live at /designs (concepts I–R).
